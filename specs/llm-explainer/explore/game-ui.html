Game UI: three directions

The real chapter-0 hero shot (slice 11b, held clock t = 5.4 s, HUD and labels off) with three different HUD skins over it. The layout, copy and behaviour are the ones the app ships. Only the form changes: panels, type, toggles, stat chips, the ladder, corner links and the pinned labels.

Mark what you'd steal or skip with the chips. Replay intro plays each direction's arrival motion (≤ 400 ms). The bar at the bottom builds your reply.
